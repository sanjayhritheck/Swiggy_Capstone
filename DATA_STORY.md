# Swiggy Cuisine Performance Diagnostic — Data Story

## Performance Summary

The analysis compares delivered-order revenue across six Swiggy cuisines against their monthly revenue targets. Three of the six cuisines exceeded their targets: North Indian, South Indian, and Italian. The remaining three cuisines—Chinese, Fast Food, and Desserts—were below target.

### Cuisine Performance

* **North Indian:** ₹216,297 revenue against a ₹180,000 target, exceeding target by **₹36,297**.
* **Chinese:** ₹127,840 revenue against a ₹140,000 target, below target by **₹12,160**.
* **South Indian:** ₹63,417 revenue against a ₹50,000 target, exceeding target by **₹13,417**.
* **Fast Food:** ₹55,123 revenue against a ₹60,000 target, below target by **₹4,877**.
* **Desserts:** ₹19,694 revenue against a ₹25,000 target, below target by **₹5,306**.
* **Italian:** ₹14,735 revenue against a ₹10,000 target, exceeding target by **₹4,735**.

Overall, **3 out of 6 cuisines met or exceeded their revenue targets**.

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize North Indian for further category investment.** North Indian generated ₹216,297, which is ₹36,297 above its ₹180,000 target—the largest positive variance among the six cuisines. The category team should prioritize catalog and assortment efforts here while monitoring whether the strong performance continues.

2. **Review the Chinese category for improvement opportunities.** Chinese generated ₹127,840 against its ₹140,000 target, leaving the largest shortfall among the below-target cuisines at ₹12,160. The category team should review the Chinese restaurant assortment and identify opportunities to improve revenue toward the target.

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides a consistent view of cuisine performance using the same monthly revenue dataset carried through SQL, the spreadsheet cross-check, and Tableau. North Indian is the strongest performer by revenue and positive variance, while Chinese has the largest revenue shortfall among the below-target cuisines.
